PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PusoAI may collect, use, store, and share information when you visit the website, browse the gallery, comment, submit content, contact us, or use AI image generation. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government identity numbers, or other sensitive or confidential information.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, we may receive a display name and comment text, an image or video and its caption or prompt, an AI text prompt, a reference image selected for AI processing, or the information you include in an email. We use this information to operate the requested feature, review submissions, moderate the community, respond to enquiries, and maintain the website.

2. AI image generation

When you request AI generation, your prompt and any reference image you choose are sent through the website backend to the AI service configured for that request. Depending on the site's configuration, this may include Cloudflare Workers AI or OpenAI. Those providers may process request data under their own terms and privacy notices. A reference image selected for generation is not automatically published to the public gallery. An AI result is intended to remain private unless you separately submit it and the site owner approves it for publication.

3. Gallery submissions and comments

Submissions are sent for review and are not intended to appear publicly before approval. If a submission is approved and published, the image or video and its public caption, prompt, or creator display name may be visible to visitors and may be shared or downloaded. Comments and the display name attached to them may also be public. Other people may save or copy public material; removing it from PusoAI cannot retrieve copies already made by others.

4. Website operation, analytics, and browser storage

To operate the site and understand basic usage, PusoAI may process technical request information and event records such as page views, gallery-item views, likes, downloads, event times, and related item identifiers. The site may use Supabase for database and storage features. Your browser may also store settings or identifiers, such as preferences, likes, a display name, or a session identifier. Hosting, database, network, and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device, and request data under their own practices.

5. Cookies and advertising

PusoAI may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, local storage, or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, including based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites, subject to the visitor's settings and applicable requirements. Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and review Google Ads Settings to manage Google ad-personalization settings. Your browser also provides controls for cookies and site storage. Those controls may affect how some site features work.

Where advertising consent is legally required, the site and its advertising partners must handle consent and ad delivery in accordance with applicable requirements. Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

6. Service providers and international processing

The website may rely on GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for database and storage features, AI providers for image generation, and Google or other advertising partners for ads. These providers may process information in countries other than the country where a visitor lives and are subject to their own privacy notices, terms, and safeguards.

7. Retention, security, and your choices

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the website, review submissions, moderate comments, respond to requests, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you supplied. Please identify the relevant image, comment, or submission. We will review requests under applicable law and may need to retain certain records for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons.

8. Children's privacy

PusoAI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Do not submit personal information about a child unless you are legally authorized to do so.

We may update this policy when the website or applicable requirements change. The updated version and date will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact pusoai001@gmail.com or visit Contact Us.
